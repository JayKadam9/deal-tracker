About this list

In-stock Amazon.in deals that cost at least ₹1,000, sit at least 10% below BuyHatke’s historical average, and are rated 4.0 or higher. Unrated products are left out. The same product appears only once.

The average is BuyHatke’s history average, not a strict 30-day mean. A drop of 80% or more can come from an inflated average rather than a typical selling price.
